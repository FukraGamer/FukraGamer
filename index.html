Intense custom room battles. The team that reaches the target round wins first claims the reward.

Format4v4 Clash Squad

Winning Reward160 FP

Frequently Asked Questions

Are emulators allowed?

No, Emulators are strictly prohibited across all games. Only mobile devices (and iPads where specified) are allowed to maintain fair competition.

How will I get Room ID and Password?

The Room ID and Password will be shared on your registered WhatsApp number and on our Discord server exactly 15 minutes before the match starts.
