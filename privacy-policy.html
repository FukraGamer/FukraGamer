Effective Date: July 20, 2026

Terms of Service

FukraGamer.World is a skill-based esports platform. Participation is conducted using Fukra Points (FP). FP has no real-world monetary value and cannot be withdrawn or converted to cash. Our platform is strictly for recreational and competitive esports.

Age Restriction: Users must be 18 years of age or older to register, use this application, and participate in any tournaments.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. We collect data (Name, In-game ID, Device info) only to improve matchmaking, prevent cheating, and ensure a fair environment. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and associated data. To delete your account:

  • Open a support ticket on our official Discord server.
  • Or contact us directly at: fukradriver.com

Upon request, all your personal data, gameplay history, and FP balance will be permanently erased from our active servers within 7-14 business days.

Rules of Conduct

  • Cheating, hacking, or using unauthorized software (including emulators) leads to a permanent ban.
  • Multiple accounts are strictly prohibited.
  • FukraGamer.World reserves the right to suspend accounts that violate community rules.

For support, contact us at: fukradriver.com
